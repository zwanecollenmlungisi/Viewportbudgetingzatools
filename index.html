Statement of financial position (balance sheet)

As at period end. Prepared in accordance with IFRS for SMEs (SA GAAP). PPE at net book value after straight-line depreciation per IAS 16.

Statement of cash flows

Direct-method cash flow presentation per IAS 7. Cash basis: income when received/recorded, expenses when paid, asset purchases as investing outflows.

Company details (used on printed reports)

Tax settings

SBC scale: 0% up to R95 750 · 7% to R365 000 · 21% to R550 000 · 28% above. When SBC is switched off, the flat company rate is used. Always confirm current rates with SARS or your accountant.

Balance sheet setup (snapshots at period end)

Manual snapshots for the statement of financial position. Update these whenever the underlying position changes. The balance sheet must satisfy: assets = equity + liabilities.

Partner capital is taken from the Registers tab. VAT and income tax payable are calculated from the current period. If the balance check shows a difference, adjust opening cash, opening retained earnings or partner capital first. If you're VAT-registered, note that the tool treats recorded amounts as either net of VAT (exclusive) or gross (inclusive) based on the "Amounts include VAT" setting — reconcile via these fields if needed.
